Our story

English House began in 2006 with one aim: help people in Vizianagaram speak English without fear.

Mr. Shiva Ganivada, Founder and Chief Coach, has about 20 years of teaching experience. He also writes the course material himself, so every class follows one clear method.

Since then we have trained 20,000+ students: school and college students, homemakers, working professionals and business people, in our classroom and online.

Questions people ask
